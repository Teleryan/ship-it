# SHIP IT! Playtest 1 (rules locked)

Version string in the app: **Playtest 1 · A rules · 9 Nov 2026**. Do not change rules during the test week; log ideas, fix only bugs. (Exception made before the first session: instant builds, see below.)

## Locked rules (version A)
- 2-5 players, 12 rounds: Concept R1-3, Production R4-8, Polish R9-11, Launch R12.
- 3 starting Juniors. 1 free workstation (desk); desks 2-4 cost 2G each, 5th 3G, 6th 4G (max 6). Bought desks stay for the phase and reset when a new phase starts (R4, R9, R12).
- Income 7 Gold a round at every player count.
- Systems print their real VP on the card (3 or 6). No synergy scoring.
- Instant builds (config toggle `instantBuild`, on): a system works the moment it is built, no build time and no crew locked. There is no Crunch. R&D gives 2 skills of your choice. Set `instantBuild` to 0 in Data Studio for the old 1-3 round builds.
- Training: a shared board tile in Concept, Production and Polish (config `trainTile`, on). One Junior per player per round becomes a Senior at once. Uses a workstation, costs 1G or 1 skill of the worker's role, +1 Culture; no skill earned, no fatigue or Burnout, no recovery. Manage-panel train and promote are off.
- Team Event: 3G, two workers, -3 Burnout.
- Launch board: Party (merged Press/Platform/Ship), Launch Crew, Keynote, Convert, plus the usual Perks. Polish: Contract and Playtest 1 removed, Plan 1 and Test 2 narrowed.
- Score band used for balance work: winners 33-63 VP.

## Bot opponents
Choose a skill per bot (menu: "Bot skill", online lobby: dropdown beside each bot seat). Presets run from Easy to Hardest. The tutorial Rival always plays Standard.

## Reset before the test
If Data Studio tuning was ever published to Live from the master account, it overlays these defaults. Open Data Studio and revert Live to shipped defaults before the first session, then check the version string and that desk 2 costs 2G.

## What to watch (from the sims)
1. Do first-timers understand the one-desk round 1 and press Buy workstation without prompting?
2. Does anyone take the Team Event tile, and at what Burnout?
3. Do 3 VP and 6 VP system cards read as huge next to Progress and Hype (1 VP per 3)?
4. 2P: does income 7 feel too poor, and do blowouts (10-15% in sims) feel unfair?
5. Do players keep the desk reset at R4 and R9 in mind?
6. Talent and Outsource: are they must-take first picks?
7. Are the merged Launch tiles too narrow in round 12?
8. Do players chase objectives on purpose (bots do not)?
9. Instant builds: is R&D (2 free skills) now a must-take tile?
10. Where in the tutorial do people stop reading, and which step do they get stuck on?

Game logs and analytics are posted per game; the owner reads them in the admin view.
